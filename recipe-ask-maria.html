Build an Expert · Ask

Ask Maria (the institutional-memory bot)

Your longtime coordinator is leaving in two weeks, taking twelve years of "how we actually handle things" with her. Before she goes, she picks 10–15 real threads where she's solving problems, and those become a Project her successor can ask questions of.

Sensitive data goes in
Real emails and Slack threads go in, so consent is required. Maria picks the threads, Maria redacts them (client names, personal asides, anyone who'd mind), and Maria gives the final yes. Never build this from someone's messages without them.
Information you give it

10–15 real email and Slack threads where Maria is actually solving problems, threads she chooses and she redacts, plus her handoff doc. Nobody has to write a "how Maria thinks" essay. Ten real conversations carry her judgment better than any description of it would, and she'd never find the time to write the essay anyway.

The recipe
  1. Ask Maria first, and mean it as a question
    Explain what you want to build and why, and make clear that she controls every thread that goes in. If she says no, this recipe ends here. If she says yes, block half a day on her calendar before her last week. This is real handoff work and should be scheduled like it.
  2. Maria picks 10–15 threads where she's solving problems
    Look for judgment in action rather than announcements: the site-visit scheduling mess, the vendor who needed a firm but warm email, the funder report that almost went out wrong. She copies each into a doc and redacts as she goes, removing client names, personal asides, and anything a colleague wouldn't want preserved. Her threads will mention other people, including clients, colleagues, and partners. Before anything is uploaded, have whoever handles HR or compliance skim the selected threads. That step is required if you work in health, childcare, or social services.
  3. Create the Project and upload her picks
    In your AI chat, go to Projects → New project and name it "Ask Maria." Upload the redacted threads using Add content, one file per thread, each named for the problem it covers, such as "site-visit-scheduling.pdf." Add her handoff doc as well. Maria gets a final look at the file list, since her consent covers exactly what's in it.
  4. Paste these Project instructions
    Open the Project's Instructions box and paste this. The line telling it to refuse when her threads don't cover something matters as much as the line about her voice.

    You don't need to understand every word below. It's written for the AI to read, not for you. Swap in the [bracketed] parts and copy the rest exactly as it is.

    Project instructions 
    You are "Ask Maria" — a record of how [Maria], our longtime
[program coordinator], handled things during her [12] years here.
Your ONLY knowledge is the threads and handoff doc in this
Project, which she chose and redacted herself.

When asked how something was handled, answer in Maria's voice and
approach, grounded ONLY in what her threads actually show. Always
say which thread you're drawing from and quote the key lines, so
the reader can go look at the real exchange.

If the threads don't cover a question, say exactly that: "Maria's
threads don't cover this." Never guess what she would have done —
a wrong answer in her voice is worse than no answer.

You describe how things WERE handled; you don't decide how they
should be handled now. For current decisions, end with: "That's
how Maria approached it — the call on today's situation is
yours."
    ROLE:
You are "Ask Maria" — a record of how [Maria], our longtime
[program coordinator], handled things during her [12] years here. You are a
record, not a stand-in: you report what she did, you do not
decide what we should do now.

GOAL:
Let anyone here ask "how did we handle this?" and get Maria's
actual approach, grounded in her own words, with the original
exchange one click away.

CONTEXT:
Your ONLY knowledge is the threads and handoff doc in this
Project, which she chose and redacted herself. Nothing outside
this Project counts — not general nonprofit practice, not what
someone in her role usually does.

STEPS:
1. When asked how something was handled, search the threads for
   the closest real instance.
2. Answer in Maria's voice and approach, grounded only in what
   her threads actually show.
3. Name the thread you are drawing from and quote the key lines,
   so the reader can go read the real exchange.
4. If the threads do not cover the question, say exactly that:
   "Maria's threads don't cover this." Never guess what she
   would have done — a wrong answer in her voice is worse than
   no answer.

OUTPUT:
A short answer in her voice, the thread cited, and the key lines
quoted. When the question is about a current decision, end with:
"That's how Maria approached it — the call on today's situation
is yours."
  5. Test it together before she leaves
    Maria and her successor sit together and ask it five real questions. Maria grades the answers: "yes, that's what I'd say," or "no, that thread was a special case." Any gaps she spots become one or two more threads while she's still here to add them.
  6. Hand it to the successor as a starting point
    The successor's first months get easier. Instead of digging through a stranger's inbox, they can ask "how did we handle the site-visit scheduling mess?" and get the actual thread back. What they decide to do next is still their call.
What you walk away with

Twelve years of hard-won knowledge that doesn't walk out the door: an askable record of how Maria actually handled the hard situations, in her words, with every answer traceable to a real thread. Maria also leaves knowing exactly what was kept, because she chose it. Decide now when this Project gets archived or deleted. A year after the successor is settled is a reasonable default. Write the date down.

Consent is a step in the recipe, not a footnote. Maria picks the threads, Maria redacts them, and Maria says yes to the specific file list rather than to the general idea. Building this from someone's messages without them is an ethics problem and a quality problem both, because only Maria knows which threads show her real judgment and which were exceptions. Other people appear in those threads too, and her redaction pass protects them as much as it protects her.
