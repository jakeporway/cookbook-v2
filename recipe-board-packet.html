Team Systems · Governance

Assemble the board packet the same way every quarter

The same inputs every quarter — program numbers, the financial summary, last meeting's minutes — assembled into your board packet format on schedule. The recurring half-day of formatting becomes an hour of reviewing.

Sensitive data goes in
Internal governance documents. Program stats, financial summaries, draft minutes. Personnel and legal matters stay out — see the caution below.
Information you give it

Your last 2–3 actual board packets — the format, demonstrated not described — plus this quarter's raw inputs: program stats, the financial summary (see the finance recipe), draft minutes, and ED notes.

The recipe
  1. Create a Project named "Board packet"
    In your AI chat: Projects → New project. Add your past packets to the Project's knowledge as format examples.
  2. Paste these Project instructions
    Open the Project's Instructions box and paste this. Note the traceability rule — it's what makes ED review fast instead of a redo.

    You don't need to understand every word below — it's instructions for the AI, not for you. Copy it as-is.

    Project instructions
    You assemble our quarterly board packet in exactly the structure
of the example packets in this Project. Each quarter I give you
raw inputs. Produce:

1. A cover memo in the ED's voice, from the examples.
2. A program dashboard section using ONLY numbers from the
   inputs, each traceable to its source document.
3. A financial summary section from the finance summary I
   provide — do not recompute it.
4. A consent agenda items list.
5. Discussion items, with one-paragraph framing each.

Flag every place where this quarter's inputs are missing
something the format expects — a [MISSING: x] flag beats a smooth
guess, always. Never carry forward a number from a past packet as
if it were current.
  3. Each quarter: drop in the inputs, get the draft
    Start a chat inside the Project with this quarter's program stats, financial summary, draft minutes, and ED notes.
  4. ED reviews every page
    Numbers checked against their sources, framing checked against reality. Fix what needs fixing, then finalize and send.
  5. Add the final packet back to the Project
    Next quarter starts from an even better example.
What you walk away with

A packet in your board's own format, with every number traceable to a source document and every gap flagged instead of smoothed over — an hour of review standing in for a half-day of formatting.

The packet is a governance document — the board acts on it. The traceability rule is what makes ED review an hour instead of a redo. Personnel matters, legal matters, and anything privileged are drafted by humans and never enter the Project.
