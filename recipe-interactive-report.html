Turn a report into an interactive web page

For the audiences who will never open a PDF — your annual report or program overview becomes a single web page you send as a link: scrollable, readable on a phone, with your charts rendered live.

Information you give it

The finished report as a PDF, your logo, and one existing page of yours whose look you like — a screenshot is fine. That's it. No rewriting the report first.

The recipe
  1. Attach the report, your logo, and a page you like
    Open a normal chat. Attach the finished report (PDF), your logo file, and a screenshot of one existing page — your website, a past newsletter, anything whose colors and feel you want matched.
  2. Ask for a single self-contained page
    Paste this. The "keep every fact exact" line is the most important sentence in the prompt — it's what makes the review step in a minute fast instead of painful.

    You don't need to understand every word below — it's instructions for the AI, not for you. Copy it as-is.

    Prompt
    Turn this report into a single self-contained HTML page I can send
as a link.

Keep every fact and number exactly as written — do not summarize,
rewrite, or add anything. Match the colors and feel of the attached
screenshot. Make it readable on a phone. Keep the sections in the
same order as the report. Render the key numbers as simple charts.
  3. Open the file and check every number
    Double-click the file the AI hands back. Go section by section and check every number against the source report — the no-new-words rule in the prompt makes this fast, because there's nothing new to fact-check, only matching to confirm.
  4. Fix with specific asks — cap it at two rounds
    If something's off, ask for the exact change: "make the third chart a bar chart, not a pie" — not "make it better." Give yourself two rounds of revisions and stop there; a page that's 90% right after two rounds is ready to publish, not ready for a third round of tinkering.
  5. Put it at a real link
    The page can be hosted free — GitHub Pages or Netlify Drop both work — or you can just attach the HTML file itself to an email, since it opens in any browser. Ask the AI for click-by-click instructions for whichever option you pick; it can walk you through either one.
What you walk away with

A single link that opens the same report on any phone or laptop — scrollable, on-brand, and checked number by number against the source. No PDF viewer required.

The risk here isn't code — it's drift. A "helpful" rewrite of your numbers is the failure mode that matters. The keep-every-fact-exact instruction plus your line-by-line check is what makes this publishable. Nothing goes to the public unreviewed.
