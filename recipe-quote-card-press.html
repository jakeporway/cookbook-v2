Remix Your Content · Generate

The quote-card press

A little page on your own computer that turns any quote or stat into a branded, ready-to-post graphic — type, click, download. The certificates trick, pointed at social media.

Public-facing output
These posts go straight to the public. A human reads every card before it posts — typos are forever on the internet, so this is one place to slow down even though the tool is fast.
Information you give it

Your brand colors — even just a screenshot of an old flyer works, the AI can read colors off a screenshot — your logo file, and one past post you liked. The example carries the design; you never have to describe fonts or spacing in words.

The recipe
  1. Grab the three ingredients
    A screenshot of anything already branded (a flyer, a slide, your website header), your logo file, and a favorite past graphic if you have one. Five minutes of digging through folders.
  2. Open a chat, attach the images, paste this prompt
    Swap in your colors and sizing. The attached images do the design talking.

    You don't need to understand every word below — it's instructions for the AI, not for you. Swap the [bracketed] parts; copy the rest exactly as-is.

    Prompt
    Build me a SINGLE self-contained HTML file: a quote-card
generator for social media. It runs entirely in my browser —
nothing I type is sent anywhere.

Look at the attached images and match [our colors, fonts as
close as web-safe fonts allow, and general style].

The page needs:
- A box where I type a quote (or a big stat + one line of
  context)
- A field for the person's name/title
- A Download button that saves a [1080×1080] PNG

Embed our logo (attached) in the corner. Keep text big and
readable on a phone. Give me a light and a dark version I can
toggle.
  3. Save the file and double-click it
    Download the HTML file it hands back, drop it somewhere memorable (a desktop folder called "Quote Cards" is fine), and double-click to open it in your browser any time.
  4. Test-drive with a real quote, download, post
    Type in something you'd actually post, hit download, open the PNG, and check it on your phone screen before it goes anywhere near a caption box.
  5. Want a second layout? Ask for a toggle
    When you want a stat version alongside the quote version, go back to the same chat and ask it to add a style toggle to the existing file — no need to start over.
Fonts are the honest limit here. The browser can't use your exact brand font unless you have the actual font file. "Close" is usually fine for social — but if your brand manager will notice, ask the chat how to embed the real font file in the page.
What you walk away with

An always-open browser tab that replaces about 20 minutes of Canva per post — plus the certificates pattern (Recipe 04), now pointed at social media instead of a printer.
