Remix Your Content · Generate

The quote-card press

A small page on your own computer that turns any quote or statistic into a branded, ready-to-post graphic. You type, click, and download. It's the certificates recipe pointed at social media instead of a printer.

Public-facing output
These posts go straight to the public. Someone reads every card before it posts. Typos live a long time on the internet, so this is a place to slow down even though the tool is fast.
Information you give it

Your brand colors, where even a screenshot of an old flyer works, since the AI can read colors off a screenshot. Then your logo file and one past post you liked. The example carries the design, so you never have to describe fonts or spacing in words.

The recipe
  1. Grab the three ingredients
    A screenshot of anything already branded, such as a flyer, a slide, or your website header, plus your logo file and a favorite past graphic if you have one. About five minutes of looking through folders.
  2. Open a chat, attach the images, paste this prompt
    Swap in your colors and sizing. The attached images handle the design.

    You don't need to understand every word below. It's written for the AI to read, not for you. Swap in the [bracketed] parts and copy the rest exactly as it is.

    Prompt 
    Build me a SINGLE self-contained HTML file: a quote-card
generator for social media. It runs entirely in my browser, and
nothing I type is sent anywhere.

Look at the attached images and match [our colors, fonts as
close as web-safe fonts allow, and general style].

The page needs:
- A box where I type a quote (or a big stat + one line of
  context)
- A field for the person's name/title
- A Download button that saves a [1080×1080] PNG

Embed our logo (attached) in the corner. Keep text big and
readable on a phone. Give me a light and a dark version I can
toggle.
    ROLE:
You are building me a small, self-contained tool. I am not a
coder, assume I will open one file and use it.

GOAL:
A quote-card generator so anyone here can produce an on-brand
social graphic in a minute, without opening a design tool.

CONTEXT:
Look at the attached images and match [our colors, fonts as close as web-safe fonts
allow, and general style]. Our logo is also
attached. It runs entirely in my browser, and nothing I type is sent
anywhere.

STEPS:
Build a SINGLE self-contained HTML file with:
1. A box where I type a quote (or a big stat plus one line of
   context).
2. A field for the person's name and title.
3. A Download button that saves a [1080×1080] PNG.
4. Our logo embedded in the corner.
5. A light and a dark version I can toggle between.
Keep the text big and readable on a phone, since the card will mostly
be seen at thumbnail size.

OUTPUT:
One HTML file with the logo and styles embedded, working offline,
plus a note on where to change the colors later if our brand
shifts.
  3. Save the file and double-click it
    Download the HTML file it hands back and put it somewhere you'll remember, such as a desktop folder called "Quote Cards." Double-click it to open in your browser any time.
  4. Test-drive with a real quote, download, post
    Type in something you'd actually post, hit download, open the PNG, and check it on your phone screen before it goes anywhere near a caption box.
  5. Ask for a toggle if you want a second layout
    When you want a stat version alongside the quote version, go back to the same chat and ask it to add a style toggle to the existing file. There's no need to start over.
Fonts are the real limitation here. The browser can't use your exact brand font unless you have the actual font file. A close match is usually fine for social media, but if your brand manager will notice, ask the chat how to embed the real font file in the page.
What you walk away with

A browser tab you keep open that replaces about 20 minutes of design work per post, and a second use for the certificates pattern from Recipe 04.
