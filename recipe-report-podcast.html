Your annual report is a podcast

Drop your finished annual report into a free tool and get back a listenable two-host conversation about your year — for the supporters who will never read 40 pages, but will listen for 12 minutes on a commute.

Information you give it

The published annual report PDF — and, optionally, a one-paragraph note on what to emphasize. That's it. The report supplies everything the two AI hosts talk about.

The recipe
  1. Confirm you're holding the public version
    No draft, no donor appendix, no internal notes tucked into the PDF — just the report exactly as it went out the door.
  2. Go to notebooklm.google.com and upload the PDF
    Sign in with any Google account, click New Notebook, then upload the report. NotebookLM is a free Google research tool; its Audio Overview button turns whatever you upload into a two-host audio conversation about it.
  3. Before generating, click customize and paste the focus prompt
    This tells the two hosts what to spend their 10 minutes on, instead of wandering the whole document evenly.

    You don't need to understand every word below — it's instructions for the AI, not for you. Swap the [bracketed] parts; copy the rest exactly as-is.

    Prompt
    Focus on [our three biggest outcomes this year] and the
story of [one program]. Keep every number exactly as it
appears in the report. Warm but factual — no hype, no
superlatives the report doesn't use. Mention that the full
report is on our website.
  4. Generate the Audio Overview and listen to all of it
    Play it back with the report open in another window. Note anything overstated, out of order, or just wrong.
  5. If a claim is off, regenerate with a steering line
    Add something like "do not discuss X; keep every number exactly as written," and try once more. One retry, then decide whether to ship or fall back to text.
  6. Download the audio and put it to work
    Embed it in the year-end email, post it on the site, or send it straight to major donors as a personal touch.
What you walk away with

A shareable ~10-minute audio piece, plus the pattern underneath it: the AI writes and performs, you edit and approve — the same "draft-only, you send" architecture as everything else in this book.

The honesty rule for anything front-facing: you are publishing this in your org's voice. Listen to every minute; if you wouldn't say it to a funder's face, it doesn't ship.
Data note: uploads to a Big Tech tool are governed by that tool's terms — which is exactly why this recipe is only for documents that are already public.
